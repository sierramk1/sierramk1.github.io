I am currently developing an interactive donor profiling dashboard in Apache Superset for the Ann Arbor Hands-On Museum through STATCOM (Statistics in the Community).

The dashboard uses professional and organizational data to help visualize donor profiles and identify patterns that may support the museum's fundraising efforts. I am working with the project team to develop interactive visualizations and filters that allow users to explore donor characteristics and affiliations.

**Tools:** Apache Superset, data visualization, data analysis
