For my Data Science and Health Equity course, I created two Epidemiological Data Briefs examining relationships between social and demographic factors and health-related behaviors.

For each brief, I developed a research question, conducted background research, defined relevant variables, assessed the survey population, performed data analysis, and presented findings in a concise format.

### Sociodemographic Influences on Birth Control Use

This brief examined factors associated with birth control use among women ages 18–45 in New York City.

### Neighborhood SES and Physical Activity

This brief examined the relationship between neighborhood socioeconomic status and physical activity among adults in New York City.
