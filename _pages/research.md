## Research Interests

My research interests include high-dimensional data analysis, machine learning, data integration, survival analysis, and Bayesian methods, with applications in public health and medicine.

## Current Research

### Network-Based Modeling of Lipidomics Data

I am currently developing network-based statistical models to study lipid signatures associated with chronic kidney disease. This work involves high-dimensional lipidomics data and explores how relationships among lipids can inform regression modeling.

I am working with collaborators in biostatistics and nephrology to develop and evaluate these approaches across multiple cohorts.
